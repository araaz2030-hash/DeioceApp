DeioceApp Privacy Policy

DeioceApp is a local desktop application designed to help users upload videos to TikTok. We respect your privacy.

  • We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data on our servers.
  • All authentication is handled directly and securely through TikTok's official API.
  • Video files are uploaded directly from your local machine to TikTok. We do not have access to your TikTok password or personal device files.

If you have any questions, please contact the developer.
